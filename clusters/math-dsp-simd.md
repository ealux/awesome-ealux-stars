## Why This Matters

For a power systems engineer:
- **FFT & wavelets** → power quality analysis, harmonic studies
- **Optimization** → optimal power flow, economic dispatch
- **Linear algebra** → state estimation, load flow calculations
- **SIMD/GPU** → real-time simulation, large-scale grid models

The overlap between DSP and ML (whisper.cpp, ggml, flash-attention) also connects this cluster to the AI cluster.
